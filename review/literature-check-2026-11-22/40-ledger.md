# Recent additions literature check

22 November 2026.

Reviewed the two source releases 65270486cd72f61b6592994ee0460fda80c1bfbf and c80fee2309f0366d1f4ee47c0c34fe07424c8dd4 against the public main snapshot 0688cf41d45508fb4c11baa549a3a17728d97866. This pass covers all twelve newly added guides and their linked CIDP, coma, seizure, military and coverage changes.

## Findings requiring an edit

| Finding | Correction and source |
|---|---|
| The pediatric migraine ibuprofen instruction delegated the ceiling to a local formulary | Made the single-dose regimen explicit at 10 mg/kg PO, maximum 400 mg, with age, prior-dose, formulation and contraindication checks. [AAN/AHS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31529481/), [Royal Children's Hospital headache pathway](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/headache/) |
| The neuro-oncology warning recognized checkpoint inhibitor overlap toxicity but left the immediate medication and respiratory actions implicit | Added withholding further ICI pending urgent assessment, serial VC/NIF assessment when feasible and hospital/ICU escalation. Added the specialist-directed treatment route for ICI-related MG and GBS, including the setting in which corticosteroids are used. [SITC neurologic toxicity recommendations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8237720/) |
| Older coma and seizure wording called ESETT proof of equivalence | Replaced it with the trial's finding of similar success rates and no established superior agent. The primary endpoint combined seizure cessation and improved responsiveness without another antiseizure intervention. The adaptive comparison did not establish formal equivalence. [ESETT original RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31774955/) |
| Older coma text promised a good prognosis for toxic/metabolic coma, including hypoglycemia | Qualified reversibility by cause, duration and associated injury. Serial assessment and exclusion of confounders remain essential. [Prolonged hypoglycemic encephalopathy cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5440422/), [NCS prognosis guidance and confounders](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12028-023-01688-3) |
| The JTS teleconsultation citation used a superseded filename returning 404 | Linked the accessible version 1.2 in the acute weakness and deployed guides. [JTS teleconsultation CPG](https://jts.health.mil/assets/docs/cpgs/Telemedicine_Deployed_Setting_19_Sep_2023_ID94_v1.2.pdf) |
| The deployed stroke reference was described as a JTS-authored guideline | Identified it as an externally authored paper hosted by JTS, consistent with its first-page notice. [Deployed stroke paper](https://jts.health.mil/assets/docs/cpgs/Stroke_Cerebrovascular_Emergencies_Deployed_Setting_03_July_2024.pdf) |

## Review by page

| Page or linked change | Literature assessment |
|---|---|
| Focused exam and localization | Emergency routing and the exam structure are consistent with [AAN curriculum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6453766/) and [AHA/ASA acute stroke guidance](https://doi.org/10.1161/STR.0000000000000513). The localization table is a bedside synthesis. |
| Acute dizziness | Trained HINTS in the appropriate continuous syndrome, gait assessment without nystagmus, MRI/MRA for central/equivocal findings and the low-certainty early steroid option are supported by [GRACE-3](https://www.saem.org/publications/grace/grace-3). Positional testing, repositioning and reassessment are supported by [AAO-HNS BPPV guidance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28248609/). |
| Visual symptoms and facial palsy | Urgent retinal ischemia and GCA routes, vascular imaging for acute third palsy and painful Horner, and papilledema evaluation are consistent with the cited specialty guidance. The [Sullivan RCT](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa072006) used prednisolone 25 mg twice daily for ten days started within 72 hours. [Third palsy paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8727561/), [GCA guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12344528/), [IIH consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6166610/) |
| Acute weakness and GBS | Respiratory escalation, early CSF/NCS limitations, IVIG 0.4 g/kg/day for five days and severity/timing conditions match [EAN/PNS 2023](https://doi.org/10.1111/ene.16073). Its two-to-four-week IVIG consideration is a good practice point. Avoiding routine repeat IVIG for predicted poor outcome is supported by [SID-GBS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33743237/). |
| Neuropathy and entrapment | The focused lab panel matches [AAN diagnostic guidance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19056666/). CTS-6 use matches [AAOS 2024](https://www.aaos.org/aaos-home/newsroom/press-releases/carpal-tunnel-syndrome-aaos-updates-clinical-practice-guideline/). Nonopioid pain treatment and class switching match [AAN painful diabetic neuropathy guidance](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000013038). Dose and organ-function restrictions refer to the linked duloxetine label. |
| Spells, syncope and functional symptoms | Witness history, ECG and risk-based testing match [NICE CG109](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/CG109/chapter/recommendations). Diagnostic certainty, coexisting epilepsy, explanation, psychological care and medication review match the [AAN functional seizure guideline](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000214466). |
| Pregnancy and postpartum | Severe hypertension recognition and magnesium regimen match [ACOG Practice Bulletin 222](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.smfm.org/media/4542/ACOG_Gestational_Hypertension_and_Preeclampsia.pdf). Imaging and urgent reperfusion evaluation are supported by the [2026 maternal stroke statement](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41603019/). Folic acid at least 0.4 mg/day and cautious medication decisions during pregnancy match [AAN/AES/SMFM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11175651/). |
| Pediatric neurology | Simple febrile seizure scope and testing are consistent with [AAP](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2010-3318) and [CHOP pathways](https://www.chop.edu/clinical-pathway/febrile-seizures-without-neurologic-disease-clinical-pathway). Suspected spasms require urgent specialist assessment and sleep EEG under [NICE NG217 section 6.3](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng217/resources/epilepsies-in-children-young-people-and-adults-pdf-66143780239813). The AES infantile epilepsy guideline excludes infantile spasms. The separate NICE source supports that branch. Added the explicit ibuprofen ceiling. |
| Adult post–cardiac arrest care | Oxygenation, PaCO2, MAP, active temperature control, EEG and multimodal timing match [AHA 2025](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/post-cardiac-arrest-care) and [NCS 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12028-023-01688-3). Added [2026 TTM2 two-year follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41697682/) to the recovery discussion. |
| Neuro-oncology | Symptomatic metastatic edema dosing is supported by [CNS steroid recommendations](https://www.cns.org/guidelines/treatment-adults-metastatic-brain-tumors/role-of-steroids-in-treatment-of-adults-with-metas). Prompt dexamethasone 16 mg and MRI within 24 hours for suspected cord compression with neurologic findings match [NICE NG234](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng234/chapter/recommendations). The biopsy caveat matches [EHA–ESMO CNS lymphoma guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11148853/). Clarified ICI toxicity actions. |
| Deployed neurology and evacuation | Early specialist consultation, serial observations, communication contingencies and transfer capability are consistent with accessible [JTS teleconsultation guidance](https://jts.health.mil/assets/docs/cpgs/Telemedicine_Deployed_Setting_19_Sep_2023_ID94_v1.2.pdf) and the [deployed stroke paper](https://jts.health.mil/assets/docs/cpgs/Stroke_Cerebrovascular_Emergencies_Deployed_Setting_03_July_2024.pdf). Clinical movement priority remains dependent on the theater process and patient. Corrected citation and attribution. |
| Aeromedical, Guard and Reserve | Public routing is consistent with [AR-MMC mission and responsibility](https://www.usar.army.mil/Resources/Health-Wellness/Medical-Management-Center/AR-MMC-Mission/), [LOD instruction](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/124101p.pdf) and [DES process manual](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/133218v1.PDF). Case-specific aviation waiver criteria and state processes require the responsible authority. |
| Linked CIDP correction | The GBS versus acute-onset CIDP distinction is supported by [EAN/PNS](https://doi.org/10.1111/ene.16073), including progression after eight weeks or repeated treatment-related fluctuations. |
| Linked coma and seizure text | Reconciled ESETT wording and toxic/metabolic prognosis. Retained confounder clearance and multimodal post-arrest assessment. [ESETT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31774955/), [AHA post-arrest](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/post-cardiac-arrest-care) |
| Deployment additions and military cross-links | Pregnancy/cancer screening matches the accessible [MOD 18 Tab A](https://api.army.mil/e2/c/downloads/2025/12/11/aa159f14/mod18-tab-a-amplification-of-the-minimal-standards-of-fitness-for.pdf). The Army postpartum deferment is documented by [Army Directive 2025-02](https://h2f.army.mil/Portals/141/pdfs/ARN43152-ARMY_DIR_2025-02-000-WEB-1.pdf) and the [official Army benefits explanation](https://myarmybenefits.us.army.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Maternity-%28Pregnancy%29-Care?serv=124). |
