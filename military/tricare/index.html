    TRICARE — getting the treatment to the patient¶

    Coverage sources checked: 22 November 2026

    One-liner: first identify the exact product, strength, indication and benefit. “FDA-approved,” “formulary,” “no PA” and “deployable” answer four different questions. The fax machine, regrettably, still has opinions.

    Start here¶

    1. Open the TRICARE Formulary Search for the exact strength/form, beneficiary and pharmacy venue. Record the result and date; retrieve the linked current PA and medical-necessity forms.
    2. Separate pharmacy benefit (Express Scripts) from medical benefit (regional contractor/authorized treatment site). An infusion or clinician-administered drug may need a medical authorization even if absent from the pharmacy PA list. Home self-injection may use a different pathway from the vial.
    3. Distinguish PA (coverage criteria), nonformulary medical necessity (MN), quantity limits, referral and site-of-care approval. One approval does not automatically satisfy the others.
    4. For an active-duty Service member, nonformulary drugs require medical necessity under the current pharmacy rules. The retrieved PA forms direct the prescriber to submit to Express Scripts first even when approval appears unlikely. Subsequent reconsideration may be available through the appropriate MTF. For other beneficiaries, a nonformulary drug may be available at network/mail pharmacies at higher cost without MN, while PA still applies; MN matters for MTF access or reduced cost share. Confirm the specific plan and venue.
    5. Submit the current form/electronic PA with the needed records. Save the approval dates, quantity, route, indication and renewal requirements. Do not delay an emergency treatment while pursuing routine outpatient authorization.

    Headache — current forms actually retrieved¶

    The following is a 21 November 2026 snapshot, not a promise of approval. Pharmacy history can satisfy some automated rules; a manually completed form may still be needed when that history is absent or incomplete.

    Product / indication Verified access rule What the current manual form asks for
    Emgality 120 mg migraine formulation Uniform formulary; no PA displayed in the live lookup Exact migraine product matters. Do not apply the cluster formulation’s PA to the 120 mg pens. Check loading and dispensing quantities
    Emgality 100 mg ×3, episodic cluster Formulary with PA Adult; neurologist prescribing/consultation; not pregnant/trying; episodic cluster; failure/intolerance/contraindication to verapamil or topiramate or lithium; no concurrent preventive CGRP therapy. Initial 6 months, renewal documents ≥50% reduction in weekly attacks
    Ubrelvy 50/100 mg acute Formulary with PA/history-based rule Adult; neurologist/consultation; failure after 2 months each of two listed triptans (sumatriptan, rizatriptan, zolmitriptan, eletriptan), or documented intolerance/contraindication; no other gepant. Form shows no expiration after approval
    Qulipta, prevention Formulary with PA/history-based rule Adult; neurologist/consultation; chronic migraine, or qualifying episodic frequency/disability below; no other gepant; failure/intolerance/contraindication to one drug from two oral preventive classes AND one injectable CGRP preventive, with the form’s 2-month trials. Initial 6 months, response-based renewal
    Nurtec ODT, acute Nonformulary + PA; MN is a separate decision PA: adult, neurologist/consultation, no other gepant, two listed triptans plus Ubrelvy failed/intolerable/contraindicated. MN: Ubrelvy and three formulary triptans documented separately. Do not confuse the PA’s two-triptan requirement with the MN form’s three
    Nurtec ODT, episodic prevention Nonformulary + PA; MN separate Qualifying episodic migraine; two oral classes + one injectable CGRP preventive + Qulipta, each with required trial/contraindication/intolerance evidence. The current PA form has an exception to the Qulipta trial for patients already stable on Nurtec prevention. Initial 6 months, response-based renewal; MN asks for Qulipta + injectable preventive history
    Aimovig / Ajovy Official list has a 365-day prior-use/history PA rule; live Aimovig result directs users to preferred Emgality 120 mg Do not label them universally “no PA.” Current manual criteria were not successfully retrieved for this review; obtain the exact current form before telling the patient how many failures qualify
    Zavzpret Official October list: PA required; nonformulary See the dated DoD committee criteria below and obtain the current PA and MN forms

    Episodic preventive thresholds: both forms accept 4–7 migraine days/month for 3 months plus MIDAS >11 or HIT-6 >50. The alternative on Qulipta is 8–14 days/month for 3 months. The May 2026 Nurtec form says at least 8 days/month for 3 months, within a confirmed episodic-migraine preventive indication. Qulipta separately has a chronic-migraine pathway.

    Preventive renewal: the retrieved Qulipta/Nurtec forms accept ≥50% reduction in monthly headache days or specified functional improvement: MIDAS baseline 11–20 falls ≥5 points; baseline >20 falls ≥30%; HIT-6 falls ≥5; MPFID falls ≥5. Nurtec acute renewal asks for a positive clinical response. These are payer criteria, not the only ways a patient can benefit clinically.

    Quantities are separate from treatment safety. The live 100 mg Ubrelvy result showed 16 tablets/30 days and 48/90 days at mail/MTF; Nurtec showed 8/30 days acute versus 16/30 days preventive. Individual authorization and dispensing edits may differ. A prescription quantity limit is not evidence that more frequent clinical exposure is safe.

    Botulinum toxin — medical benefit and regional criteria¶

    TRICARE’s coverage policy distinguishes chronic migraine from episodic migraine, cluster, tension-type and other daily headaches. A PTH or NDPH phenotype does not automatically create a covered indication. Establish the actual diagnosis; do not relabel a patient to obtain payment.

    Region / source Practical documentation
    West — policy revised 20 August 2025, retrieved November 2026 Adult; ≥15 headache days/month for >3 months, ≥8 with migraine features and ≥4-hour headache duration; at least one first-line oral preventive failed or prior botulinum treatment, and specialist involvement. Initial first treatment plus two subsequent treatments over six months; continuation requires documented improvement or maintained benefit
    East — public MP22-030E returned at review Adult; headache frequency ≥15 days/month for ≥3 months, qualifying migraine duration/features; preventive medication ineffective/not tolerated for ≥3 months. Confirm current requested product, units, procedure, follow-up authorization and continuation rules with the contractor

    The regional policies are not identical. Use the current region’s authorization route rather than importing a pharmacy gepant PA or another insurer’s checklist.

    The October 2026 files confirm the restrictions listed above. These older committee records give details useful for preparing documentation. Confirm them against the current form before using a specific restriction, trial length, or renewal threshold.

    Drug What the dated official criteria requested
    Zavzpret Adult acute migraine, neurologist prescribing or consultation, no concurrent small-molecule CGRP drug, and failed/intolerable/contraindicated intranasal sumatriptan AND either Nurtec or Ubrelvy. Initial 6 months, then document clinical benefit. Confirm current PA and separate nonformulary MN. August 2023 DoD minutes
    Evrysdi Neurologist, documented SMN1 molecular diagnosis, ≥2 SMN2 copies in this version, SMA type, weight and dose, reproductive counseling when applicable, and restrictions on concurrent nusinersen and advanced ventilator dependence/paralysis. Initial 6 months, then documented improvement or stabilization for annual renewal. The 2023 age/dose table predates later label changes.     Briviact tablet or oral solution October claims rule references prior brivaracetam or levetiracetam use within 180 days. The May 2024 manual criteria specified neurologist prescribing, focal-onset seizures, and insufficient response, intolerance or contraindication to levetiracetam. This version did not expire. Confirm the exact formulation and current rule. May 2024 DoD minutes
    Zilbrysq The 2024 criteria asked for adult AChR-positive gMG, neurologist prescribing, prior pyridostigmine, steroid-sparing treatment and FcRn antagonist history, vaccination documentation, and no concurrent FcRn/C5 therapy. Initial 6 months, with annual renewal based on documented benefit. Separate payer requirements from the current FDA vaccination and REMS requirements. May 2024 DoD minutes
    Nuplazid Adult hallucinations or delusions due to Parkinson disease psychosis, neurologist/psychiatrist/geriatric specialist involvement, attempted adjustment of PD medicines, QT/arrhythmia assessment, and no additional antipsychotic. The 2024 revision shows an MMSE item being edited. Confirm the current form before applying a cognitive cutoff. This version listed no PA expiration. November 2024 DoD minutes
    Firdapse The published records requested neurologist/oncologist prescribing and laboratory evidence of LEMS. Older records predate the pediatric label and include historical Ruzurgi language. Use the current form for age, step therapy and renewal. February 2019 DoD minutes

    Build a usable authorization packet¶

    • Beneficiary/plan/region and prescriber details; exact generic + brand, strength, formulation, route, dose, quantity/day supply, indication and requested site.
    • Diagnostic evidence relevant to the requested drug: e.g., headache diary/MIDAS/HIT-6; seizure type; AChR/AQP4 results; electrodiagnostic CIDP evidence; amyloid confirmation and baseline MRI; genetic diagnosis. Include only what the form and clinical indication require.
    • A treatment table with drug, dose, start/stop dates, duration, adherence, response, adverse reaction, and specific reason a contraindicated option cannot be used. “Failed everything” is not documentation.
    • Label-required safety testing, vaccines/REMS where applicable, and a monitoring plan. These clinical requirements and payer step therapy are different things.
    • Continuation evidence using the same baseline measure and actual response; approval dates and impending renewal deadline.
    • If denied, read the reason: wrong benefit/site, missing data, nonformulary MN and lack of coverage are different problems. Use the plan’s reconsideration/appeal route with the relevant records and evidence.

    Specialty pharmacy: initiation and renewal¶

    These are dated DoD criteria for packet preparation. Confirm the current form and prior approval in the live tool before submission. A new indication or device on the FDA label can precede an updated payer form. Use the exact presentation. Pharmacy rules for a prefilled syringe cannot be carried over to an infusion.

    Product Initial documentation in the cited source Continuation in that version
    Vyvgart Hytrulo prefilled syringe, 1,000 mg/5 mL August 2025 minutes: adult, neurologist, CIDP or AChR-positive gMG. For gMG the record required inadequate response or intolerance to pyridostigmine and a steroid-sparing agent, plus a restriction on concurrent FcRn/C5 treatment. Recheck serostatus because subsequent FDA labeling changed. Committee minutes, Appendix C 6 months initially. Prior approval and documented improvement and stabilization to justify continued treatment.
    Austedo IR/XR and Ingrezza November 2023 minutes: adult, indication-specific specialist and diagnosis. TD requires documented functional impact, mood/suicide assessment and consideration of changing the culprit drug. For Huntington chorea, check the tetrabenazine prerequisite in the current form. Committee minutes, Appendix C 1 year initially. Then prior approval and treatment response, including AIMS improvement of at least 2 points for TD or clinician-assessed improvement for Huntington chorea, with continued mood/suicide monitoring.
    Radicava ORS August 2022 record: adult with ALS, neurologist, disease duration ≤2 years, ALSFRS-R ≥2 on every item and FVC ≥80%. These are the dated payer thresholds, separate from the FDA indication. DoD August 2022 background record No expiry specified in the 2022 criteria. Confirm the current form and submit dated baseline measures.
    Kesimpta November 2020 minutes: adult relapsing MS, neurologist, no active HBV or concomitant disease-modifying therapy. The old record also addressed previous Ocrevus failure. Check current criteria before applying any sequencing restriction. Committee minutes, Appendix C No expiry specified in the 2020 criteria. Confirm current approval terms and the correct product.
    Enspryng May 2025 committee recommendation, published in the 2026 advisory packet: adult, neurologist, documented AQP4-positive NMOSD and attack dates. The recommendation counted the presenting attack and required at least two in two years, with at least one in the preceding year. Check the current form for its safety, concurrent-biologic and preferred-product questions. Advisory packet, pp. 16–17 No expiry in the recommendation. Confirm the implemented criteria and approval terms with the pharmacy benefit.
    Leqembi IQLIK maintenance November 2025 minutes: specialist, MCI or mild dementia due to Alzheimer disease and documentation of 18 months of initial IV lecanemab. That record predates the 500-mg SC initiation option. Confirm the initiation and maintenance packs separately. Committee minutes, Appendix C No expiry specified for that maintenance form. Confirm current terms and provide baseline clinical stage, therapy dates and safety monitoring when requested.

    Start the renewal from the approval letter. Record the expiration date, approved product and units, site of care, and whether a change in dose, formulation or site needs a new request. Attach the baseline and current score using the same instrument, along with adverse effects, missed doses and relevant safety monitoring. If the current product form is missing, call the pharmacy benefit team for it. Ask for initial and continuation criteria separately.

    Medical benefit, East botulinum, and US Family Health Plan¶

    Route What to request
    East medical benefit Use Humana Military provider referrals and authorizations and the plan's current policy. The public MP22-030E botulinum policy specifies chronic migraine eligibility. Request separate reauthorization instructions from East. Send baseline versus current headache/migraine days, response, treatment dates, dose/units and requested service period.
    West medical benefit Confirm drug/HCPCS, diagnosis, site and whether the West medical list or Express Scripts applies. Use TriWest prior authorization resources and the current drug or regional policy. Apply West’s botulinum continuation criteria only to West.
    US Family Health Plan Identify the designated USFHP organization from the official service-area directory. Use its pharmacy network and its own medication PA, renewal and specialty-referral forms. Direct the request through the member’s designated plan. Express Scripts and military pharmacy routing do not apply to USFHP prescriptions.
