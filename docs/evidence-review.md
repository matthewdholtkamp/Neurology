# Evidence and update policy

**Review history: initial site-wide pass 21 November 2026; independent correction pass 22 November 2026.** This site-wide review covered all **59 existing Markdown pages** in the source snapshot, including **56 clinical pages**, the military framework, home and contributing pages. Three new reference pages were added: TRICARE, deployment/medications and this review policy.

## What “reviewed” means here

The text was read for dangerous instructions, internal contradictions, outdated guidelines, drug-label changes, unsupported comparative claims and military/coverage assumptions. Material corrections were checked against accessible primary guidelines, trials, FDA labels and official DoD/TRICARE sources. Evidence briefs distinguish trial findings, observational data and clinical interpretation where those distinctions change a decision.

This is an **AI-assisted editorial/clinical evidence review**, not independent clinician certification of every sentence or a systematic review of every drug on the site. Some cited papers were accessible only as abstracts or partial full text. An automated FDA-label screen supported, but did not replace, manual review; it does not validate every dose, combination or off-label regimen. Historical statements that every dose had been “verified” through AI answer services have been removed.

The first proposal was **not ready to publish unchanged**. ## How to use a clinical page

The two subsequent releases added twelve guides, including the aeromedical and Reserve pathway. A focused literature recheck on 22 November covered those guides, the linked CIDP and coma changes, and the seizure treatment text used by the new pathways. The record in `review/literature-check-2026-11-22/40-ledger.md` maps each page to sources and findings. Corrections clarify pediatric dosing, checkpoint inhibitor toxicity, trial interpretation, reversibility of coma, and deployed-source attribution and access. Specialty coverage and case-specific duty determinations remain subject to the stated source limits.

- Start with red flags and the first actions. Drug doses are generally **adult** unless a pediatric regimen is explicitly identified; do not scale an adult order set to a child.
- Check indication, route, organ function, pregnancy, interactions and local administration protocol before ordering. A table is not an individualized prescription.
- New or conflicting safety information should prompt review of the current product label and relevant specialist guidance.
- Use the [TRICARE page](military/tricare.md) for the exact coverage scope and forms retrieved. A missing form is marked as unresolved; clinical step therapy is not invented.
- Use the [deployment guide](military/deployment-medications.md) for the **CENTCOM-specific** snapshot and the [retention guide](military/deployability.md) for the separate Service framework.

## Updating the site

For each material recommendation, record the source/version, applicable population, action, important exclusions and review date. Prefer the issuing society, trial report, FDA label or official policy over summaries of summaries. Cite the source beside the recommendation or in a clearly matching reference entry.

When a new guideline differs from an older trial, explain which population or decision changed. Do not label a negative study as supportive, a pending application as approval, or a single observational comparison as proof of superiority. Replace a definitive instruction with a bounded recommendation when uncertainty matters.

For humor, aim at bureaucracy and clinical absurdity. Keep doses, resuscitation instructions, uncertainty and vulnerable patients out of the punchline. Put the useful action before the joke; the person reading this may be holding a pager and a sandwich of uncertain provenance.

**Before publication:** a clinician should review the clinical changes, the remaining source limits and any locally applicable policy. Preview the pages and check links. Publishing requires the site owner’s explicit authorization; pushing the repository updates the public site.
