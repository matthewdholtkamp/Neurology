# By presentation

<!-- lint: nonclinical -->

Start with what the patient, nurse, or medic tells you. Open the relevant guide for the first bedside checks, when to escalate, and what to document before calling for help.

| What are you seeing? | Start here |
|---|---|
| New neurologic finding without a clear localization | [Focused neuro exam and localization](neuro-exam.md) |
| New dizziness, spinning, or severe imbalance | [Acute dizziness and vertigo](dizziness.md) |
| Vision loss, double vision, unequal pupils, or facial weakness | [Vision loss, double vision, and facial palsy](neuro-ophthalmology.md) |
| Weakness over hours to days, weak cough, or trouble swallowing | [Acute weakness and Guillain–Barré syndrome](acute-weakness.md) |
| Burning feet, numb fingers, or foot drop | [Peripheral neuropathy and entrapment](peripheral-neuropathy.md) |
| Blackout, shaking, or recurrent unexplained spells | [Spells, syncope, and functional neurologic disorder](spells.md) |

## Time-critical pathways

| Immediate concern | Open |
|---|---|
| Sudden focal deficit | [Acute ischemic stroke](../stroke/index.md) |
| Ongoing or recurrent convulsions without recovery | [Status epilepticus](../seizure/index.md#status-epilepticus-the-timed-escalation-ladder-the-code) |
| Declining consciousness | [Coma workup](../neurocritical-care/coma-workup.md) |
| Thunderclap headache | [Headache triage](../headache/index.md) |
| Possible spinal cord or cauda equina syndrome | [Acute myelopathy](../acute-myelopathy.md) |
| Suspected meningitis or encephalitis | [Neuroinfections](../neuroinfections/index.md) |
