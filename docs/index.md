# Neuro Scutbook

A bedside neurology reference for **MDs, NPs, PAs, and medics** — how to work up and manage a
patient with neurological symptoms in the clinic, ER, or on the wards, the way a neurology consult
would. Every topic pairs a prescriptive **consult workflow** with a plain-language **evidence
brief** and a standardized **military-medicine layer**.

## How to use this

- **On call or at the bedside?** Open the topic and read top-to-bottom: red flags → rapid
  approach → workup → **tiered Step 1→2→3 order sets with doses** → disposition.
- **Prescribing?** Look for the **TRICARE** box wherever drugs appear — preferred versus
  PA-gated, and what the authorization needs.
- **Studying or teaching?** Jump to the **Discover** brief at the bottom of each page for the
  evidence and the key trials.
- **Search** — press `/` anywhere and type a symptom, drug, or trial name.
